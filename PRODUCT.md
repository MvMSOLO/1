# NEXUS Product Vision

## The Product Thesis
The web contains vast amounts of useful information, but mainstream engines over-emphasize SEO popularity, duplicate claims, and force users to scan endless links or wade through fake "AI-generated" chat dialogues.

NEXUS focuses on **Usefulness for This Query**. We extract public evidence, remove duplicate claims, cross-reference sources, indicate contradictions, and format the user's interface dynamically to match the specific search intent.

## Core Product Laws
1. **Instant**: Immediacy in response and transition.
2. **Zero Learning Curve**: Straightforward, intuitive layout.
3. **Usefulness > Popularity**: Heuristic composite ranking.
4. **Depth**: Search multiple public interfaces.
5. **Transparency**: Trace claims back to original sources.
6. **Honesty**: No fabrication or hallucinations; flag conflicts.
7. **Adaptive Experience**: UI adapts to matching QueryType.
8. **Progressive Discovery**: Make search results an interactive exploration path.
9. **Complexity Belongs to the Machine**: Raw details handled gracefully.
10. **Speed Without Waste**: No heavy preloading or keeping iframes alive.

## Core Target Scenarios
- **Factual Research**: Deep dive into Wikipedia, facts, and citations.
- **Comparison**: Compare systems/languages (e.g. React vs Vue) with structured tables and trade-offs.
- **Troubleshooting**: Diagnostic steps, possible causes, symptoms, and troubleshooting instructions.
